# Local SEO plan: Pakistan service areas

MRK Digital can state service areas only after the owner confirms the areas actually served. The existing Customizer Service Area field is the single source for concise site-wide wording; it should never be used to imply an unverified physical office.

## Recommended approach

- Use a natural service-area sentence on the Contact page, such as service availability for Mian Channu, Khanewal, Kabirwala, Multan, Burewala, Pakistan and remote clients **only where true**.
- Add local context to a service page only when it changes the advice, process, availability, delivery method or practical requirements.
- Prioritize useful content such as website requirements for businesses in a particular market or remote consultation process, rather than duplicate “service in city” pages.
- Keep NAP details absent until the owner supplies verified business contact details. Do not invent an office address, telephone number or registration information.

## Candidate pages requiring owner evidence before publishing

- Web Developer in Mian Channu
- WordPress Developer in Khanewal
- Website Development in Multan
- PLC Programming Services in Pakistan
- Arduino Automation Projects in Pakistan

A page may proceed only when its local details are unique, substantive and owner-approved. Otherwise, target the location naturally through the relevant main service or guide instead.
